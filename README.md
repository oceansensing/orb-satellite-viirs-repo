# orb-satellite-viirs-repo

Two fields from the University of Delaware ORB lab's experimental VIIRS 8-day
anomaly composite over 16–52° N, 100–50° W, as gridded data at 0.04° for the
oceansensing.org ocean map: the chlorophyll-a anomaly and the sea surface
temperature anomaly, each a difference from ORB's climatology.

Checked four times a day; ORB makes one composite a day.

## Sources and credits

The University of Delaware's Ocean Remote sensing laboratory (ORB) serves its processed products openly.
